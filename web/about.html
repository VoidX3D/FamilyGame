Made for the same room

Less setup. More “wait, who wrote that?”

Family Game Hub turns a handful of phones into one shared game night. One host opens a room, everyone else joins with a short code, and the games take care of the rest.

One room, one rhythm

The host controls the playlist and the pace. Players see only what they need, with private answers and prompts kept private until the right moment.

02

Built for real phones

No account, download, or complicated invite. It works in a browser, can be installed like an app, and remembers your name and room on your device.

03

Private by default

There are no ads, tracking pixels, public profiles, or chat logs. Rooms live in memory and disappear after they have been idle.

What you can play

The hub includes quick social games, guessing games, drawing rounds, and conversation starters. A host can run one game or build a whole playlist for the evening. Between rounds, the room keeps the group together and carries the score forward.

How a game night works

  1. Open the host console and create a room.
  2. Share the five-character code or invite link.
  3. Players choose a name and join from any phone, tablet, or laptop.
  4. Start the playlist, laugh loudly, and let the room handle the turns.
